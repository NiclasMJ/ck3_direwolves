# The head cameras orbit dragon_origin, which sits between the wolf's FEET at
# z = 0 - the face is another ~94 units forward (-Z) of it, measured. So the
# radius is NOT the distance to the face: a radius that equals the face offset
# drops the camera right on top of the muzzle, side-on. Each radius/height below
# is derived from "150 units in front of the face, 14 deg off its axis, 8 deg
# above it", converted to cylindrical about the origin, then scaled per stage.

		position = cylindrical{ 145 65 -9 }
